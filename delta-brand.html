01 The Measured Edge

One mark, two readings. The internal axis is a set of yard lines and a precision scale at the same time — the product's thesis, drawn.

Delta triangle

Δ means change. Pointing up: growth, advantage, ascent — and the literal shape of your name.

Graduated axis

Hash marks that widen toward the base read as field yard lines and a measurement scale — precision, the range opening up.

Two-facet fold

Lit plane and shadow plane give quiet depth without a heavy 3D render — dimensional, still flat enough to scale.
